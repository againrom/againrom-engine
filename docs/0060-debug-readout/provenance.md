# Provenance — 0060 debug readout

## Backing

**Empty, and that is the finding.** No row of this story's contract rests on a decoded fact. The
readout reports quantities this project already computes — a clock period, a tick number, a digest,
a cell, an entity's own speed input — and it asserts nothing whatever about the original. Nothing
was cited, nothing needed to be, and no research item was opened.

One decoded fact is *visible through* the readout without being claimed by it, and it is worth
naming so a reader does not go looking for a defect. A freshly opened map runs at the game's own
shipped whole-millisecond quotient for its speed index — 62 ms, widened to 62000 µs — where our own
rate model at the same stated rate computes 62500 µs. Both are already in the tree and neither is
this story's; the readout simply makes the difference legible, which is what a readout is for.

## Ours by choice

| What the spec fixes | Why it is ours |
|---|---|
| **The whole of it — FR-1.** The readout is a **development instrument**, authored end to end. | Nothing published describes a parameter readout in the original, and none was looked for: this is our seat at the machine, not a reconstruction. **AUTHORED** — a verdict, not a hold. |
| FR-3 — which nine values are stated, their wording, their order and their units | Diagnostic design. The cadence is the owner's request; the rest earn the key by being the state a determinism bug is diagnosed from. |
| FR-6 — the cursor line states the **ground pick's** cell rather than the drawn lattice's | The two disagree on relief and one had to be chosen. The pick's answer is what the game will act on, so it is the one worth showing; and showing it is the first time that divergence is observable at all. |
| FR-7, FR-8 — on by default, hidden by `F1` | On by default because a readout that must be switched on cannot report the key that switched it on. `F1` is the diagnostic register the key convention already authored, which deliberately left `F1` free for this. |
| FR-9, FR-10 — the corner, and that hidden costs nothing | Placement and cost, ours entirely. |
| The box's geometry, colours, labels and font | The unit panel's authored appearance, reused unchanged so the two boxes read as one instrument. |

## Open

Nothing about the original is left open by this story, because nothing about the original is
asserted by it.

One question **is** left open by it and is deliberately not this story's: the ground pick resolves
through the flat lattice while every glyph is placed on the lifted one, so on sloped ground the cell
the readout states and the cell the drawn lattice puts under the cursor are different cells. That is
a disclosed divergence of the pick, already recorded where the pick lives, and the readout makes it
visible without changing it.

## Removed

Nothing. No statement was dropped from this contract and none carried evidence to lose.
