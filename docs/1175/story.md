# Accepted format contracts

## Result

Adopt public knowledge k37 and apply its bounded corrections to existing
format consumers. Fresh maps honor authored zero current health for Building
placements while retaining definition maximum health and the separate Shop
arm. ALM record-header words remain raw bits. Linked Type9 head effects use
their documented byte result. SAV Player control remains a full 32-bit value.
The .16 glyph decoder stops accepting bit31-set trailers as positive counts;
.16a keeps its separate count and palette contract.

## Authority and scope

- ALM-HEADER-098 and ALM-WRITER-099 retract only the numeric interpretation of
  record-header +0x10. The payload angle and container safety checks remain.
- ALM-127/128 distinguish full-kind extension selection, stored-kind Shop
  selection, low-byte definition selection and the Building zero override.
- ALM-T9CONTROL-175 narrows only the linked head's A+43 result. Tail validation,
  operand widths and ineligible-coordinate checks retain their own contracts.
- SESS-074 and ALM-139/140 reject a universal Boolean interpretation of Player
  control. They do not establish native session-join chronology or local-player
  selection from every possible value.
- SPR16A-070 through072 establish raw .16 storage and signed indexing. The
  bounded loader refuses negative trailers; it does not emulate unchecked
  original allocation. SPR16A-TRLR-012 and SPR16A-RDR-017 still govern .16a.

## Proof

- Before the fix, three synthetic Building zero cases retained health73,
  and four non-Boolean Player words were refused. The corrected tests pass.
- Building controls cross six kinds and six raw words, retaining maximum
  health and footprint. The registered installed witness opens maps40,50,61,
  81,111,120,150 on EN/RU:39 authored-zero placements and355 ruin draw entries
  per root. None is directly referenced by the compiled structure-field nodes
  in those maps. Cold AGS retains both zero and subsequently changed health,
  with the same structure state and draw selection. These are placement
  witnesses, not39 observed progression failures.
- Header read/edit/write controls retain ten distinct raw words, including a
  signaling-NaN bit pattern. Existing writer byte preservation is retained;
  this change removes the unsupported float interpretation from its API.
- Type9 head boundaries212/213/65535 produce255/0/42 in ground and owned
  containers and native continuation. B/C remain low bytes; tail overflow and
  ineligible coordinates still refuse. Player2/256/258/0xffffffff survive two
  byte-exact format round trips; outcome3 still refuses.
- Sprite negative/zero/positive/large-count controls distinguish .16 from
  .16a. An independent intake over22 installed archives compares all1090
  member input, decoded-pixel and available text-composition receipts before
  and after: identical, with zero decode failures. These are engine hash
  comparisons, not original framebuffer parity.
