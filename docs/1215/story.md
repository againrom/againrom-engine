# Current area effects and attachments write SAV

An active area or a caster-bearing actor attachment must not prevent mission
SAVE. This is the next refusal family in the seat's `SAV-ENDGAME.md`, M6.
Its Rules for every story section governs this work: current state first,
no new refusal or AGS fallback, one current producer, EN/RU ordinary SAVE and
cold LOAD/continuation/next-SAVE proof, and no census regression.

The producer writes the current area root, its Effect or Effect_DirectDamage
child, independent countdown and ring phase, and six current cell-layer links.
Overwritten clouds retain their independent clocks even with empty coverage.
Actor attachments retain current kind, mode, magnitude and remaining time.
Cold LOAD does not repeat their initial application or cast payment.

MAGIC-AREATICK-036, MAGIC-AREAAPPLY-038, MAGIC-CLOUDCLOCK-154,

construction; Effect's complete serializer likewise has no caster field.

original LOAD convention clears it, so caster attribution after SAV LOAD is
