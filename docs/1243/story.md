# Offer first-part continuation

## Result

A readable shop or school offer constructs a modal panel even when part 1 is
absent, conditionally rejected or lacks a following header LF. The panel keeps
`Nothing to say`. Normal construction registers the head offer, removes its
stable label, selects its mission, announces its campaign record and records
the existing picture-bearing marker seam. The next command attempts part 2.
A later failed attempt closes the panel once. It does not replace the retained
text, accepted tag, face or speech source.
