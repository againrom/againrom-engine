# DIVERGENCES-CLOSED.md — settled rows

Rows moved out of `DIVERGENCES.md` when their status became `CLOSED`: the implementation now matches
researched ROM1 behaviour, or the claim the row leaned on was retracted. Split out on the owner's
instruction of 2026-08-17, when the single ledger had reached 105 rows and 12 of them were closed.

A closed row is a record and is not destroyed. It carries what was once divergent, what settled it,
and the claims that were authoritative at the time. Columns and types are `DIVERGENCES.md`'s; ids
come from the same sequence and are never reissued.

Nothing is appended here directly. A row is written in `DIVERGENCES.md`, and it arrives here only by
being closed.
