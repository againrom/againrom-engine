# Imported fighter training and SAV continuation

## Result

An imported city fighter can buy a school level, save through ordinary SAVE
as `Asg&`, reload in a fresh process, and buy the next level at the maintained
base price. SAV to AGS to SAV preserves the trained state. The same current
Human values feed the town sheet and the next mission's pools and combat.

- Typed retained Human state belongs to stable party identity. Native saves

  fields. Unchanged companions, spellbooks, inventory and reference graphs
  remain in the city document. Changed unsupported state fails explicitly.

  explicit SAV export still refuses the changed member. The retained basis
  stays available for validation and cannot authorize forged state.
- Independent field expectations, ordinary SAVE dispatch, fresh-process
  conversion, repeated training and mission projection witness the result.
