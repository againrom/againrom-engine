the main-load age pass. A new main clears its latch, retains age1 children and
removes age2 children. Side completion removes its record. Gate choice checks
the main first, then child order; hearing alone supplies no latch. Current
SAV emits the retained records rather than only current chapter candidates.
The existing economic and offer provenance paths remain in place.

A configured building's later declared main is loaded before acceptance
latches it. Fresh and restored handovers preserve the old chapter's consumed
row, load the new chapter's records and apply one child age pass. Repeated
registration uses the existing record and pays no completion reward. An
unaccepted later main remains a candidate through SAV/LOAD; it supplies no
gate latch. A historical snapshot's accepted later main recovers this record
rather than availability alone. Invalid rows cannot report successful handover
without a record; their native consumption semantics are not established here.

Ordinary restored snapshots retain the complete Campaign projection. Missing
historical records build current candidates at age0 and accepted
previous-decade children at age1, including missing section values; completed
children are excluded. Compatible saved records keep their ages, latches and
values. If installed main definitions change or town entry resolves an older
pre-town main, records follow the resolved authored main. Snapshot validation
checks wire identity, duplication and age rather than the installed mission
population. DIV-1628 bounds the unrecoverable historical ages. Legacy fixtures now announce/clear
records rather than injecting availability without a corresponding record.
The shop fixture enters through Arrive instead of setting open on a prologue.
The world-marker fixture uses a live installed main/child pair and explicitly
labels its synthetic missing-marker control when needed.

with its resource path and creates no substitute panel. Geometry, captured

released shared dialogue routes. DIV-1627 retains native lifecycle/reporting
