## Authored where research is silent

No claim answers the question, so the implementation authored one.

| DIV-3023 | mods / a mission a guest party plays | Owner: a mod adds a mission after mission 20 in which the player plays another character alone; nothing of the hero or the party is lost, and that character then joins the party with what she gained | The original opens the town of chapter 30 after mission 20 and grants companion 22 there from her definition row; it has no mission between the two | A `data/missions.toml` declaration (`docs/MODS.md`) plays its mission after the win of mission `after`, before the town that win opens. The campaign is written as the town SAV the one producer writes at that point (after the town latch, before the shop and the grant) and kept in the session. The guest is the companion `guest` as the town of `chapter` would grant him or her over that party, leading a party of one with the declared purse. Her win carries her home through the ordinary carry, LOADs the kept SAV through the one town LOAD, and the town's own grant of that companion takes her in her carried state; a join condition of another mod holds her as it holds the definition row. A lost guest mission ends as a lost campaign mission does | DEVIATION | Owner direction | An owner decision on a second guest, a guest who stays out of the party, or a guest mission between two other boundaries | ACCEPTED |
| DIV-3024 | mods / SAV of a guest mission | Owner direction of `DIV-3023`; SAV is the only save format | The original's SAV names a campaign mission whose map its scenario container holds; it cannot know a mod mission | A SAVE inside the guest mission writes the mission natively from the current state: the head names the mod's mission number and map, the campaign records are the waiting town's (the guest mission never becomes the selected mission). LOAD refuses, naming the mod, when the mod mark differs, when no active mod declares the recorded mission under that number, or when a SAV of a mod mission number carries no guest record. Inference, not measured: the original would not find the map of the mod's mission number in its scenario container | DEVIATION | One save format and one producer | A measurement of what the original does with such a SAV | ACCEPTED |
| DIV-3025 | mods / what a guest mission carries back | Seat choice under owner direction of `DIV-3023`: nothing but the guest member's own state carries back | In the original every won mission's purse, loot, payment, fame and bestiary knowledge reach the town | The guest joins as the ordinary mission carry leaves her: experience, skills, level, spells and what she wears and carries. | DIV-3026 | mods / leaving a guest mission | Owner direction of `DIV-3023` | The original has no guest mission | The mission-abandon menu action refuses to abandon a guest mission (there is no town she entered it from); its restart reopens the mission with the guest as she started it | DEVIATION | A guest mission has no town to return to before her win | An owner decision on abandoning a guest mission | ACCEPTED |
| DIV-3141 | mods / a campaign mission played on a mod's map | Owner: a mod map is an ALM map; it plays as an additional mission, a replaced campaign mission, a standalone mission or a map for another base | The original builds campaign mission n from `scenario/<n>.alm` of its scenario container and its texts from `text/battle/m<n>/` | A `data/missions.toml` table with `kind = "replace"` (`docs/MODS.md`) serves the mod's map at `scenario/<number>.alm` and its texts, title and briefing over the installed mission's on the front end's own filesystem, through the guest mission's resolver; the mission start is the one `enterMission`. Scenario rows, world map, payment and the town that follows stay the install's. Rules: the number must be a campaign mission of the install whose map the install holds, else the mod is refused (proven on the first game; a mod names its bases in `applies-to`); a map `MapOpener` refuses is refused when the mod loads, naming file and line; one number belongs to one mod, so a second mod replacing the same mission is refused when it loads, naming the first; a guest number is never a campaign number, so the two kinds cannot collide, and a guest after a replaced mission plays after its win | DEVIATION | Owner direction | An owner decision on the standalone mission (with multiplayer) or the map for another base | ACCEPTED |
| DIV-3142 | mods / SAV of a replaced campaign mission | Owner direction of `DIV-3141`; SAV is the only save format | The original's SAV names a campaign mission whose map its scenario container holds | A SAVE inside a replaced mission writes the ordinary SAV with the mod mark (`DIV-1802`), whose content digest covers the mod's map; no record of its own is added.
