| DIV-1907 | mission menu / abandon a mission and return to town | Owner: abandon is a base-game action in both games, default on; a mod may disable the base action or replace its labels | The original game ends a mission by completion or failure, and the End Quest page offers Change map, Exit to main menu and Exit to Windows; no action returns to the town without completing the mission | The base action is data registered by App.SetModScreens, shared by both games. game.rules.abandon is 0 or 1, default 1 in rules.Defaults; 0 removes only the base action. A mod action of the same kind replaces it without a duplicate and keeps its existing mod slot. EN/RU labels are the example mod words in the engine text tables; Return remains the install word. Over a mission entered from the town and still undecided, the entry opens a page of two rows, the confirming row and the install's Return word; Return and Escape go back with nothing changed. Confirming leaves the mission without victory and returns the party to the town. The town is the town as it was entered: the mission works on copies, so the party, purse, items, hired pools and offers are those held at entry and nothing the mission gained or lost is carried out. The mission stays offered and no completion, payment or economic arrival is paid. A first-game mission restored from a SAV has no entry town, since a SAV holds the mission's party and the front end holds none: abandoning it carries the party home as it stands, through the carry a won mission uses (survivors with their loot and experience, city objects, hired pools, purse), so the town party is never empty and the town SAVE writes; hired men return to the tavern pools as at a win, and nothing is recorded as won. The quick-spell bindings are put back to those held when the mission was opened. Two entry effects remain: the mission's documents, granted at entry, stay, and the mission is the selected one. In the first game the party travels home along the world map as it does after a won mission, and the town SAVE is blocked until it arrives, as it is then. No completion movie is requested. In the second game the live campaign retains the town it departed. Abandon restores that town and its availability without completion, payment, scenario-departure writes or a movie. The entry party remains outside the mission; the same item-home builder a normal town arrival uses moves the hero pack back into the town bag. The existing bank home route releases the mission. A second-game mission LOAD retains no departure town, so it offers no abandon entry; no saved state or SAV layout changes. | DEVIATION | For a mission entered from the town the entry state is the state the front end already holds, so no snapshot is taken and none can drift from it; for a restored mission the carry is the town's own return | A snapshot of the town taken at entry, if a mission begins to write the town while it runs; a saved second-game departure-town identity would extend the loaded-mission route | ACCEPTED |
